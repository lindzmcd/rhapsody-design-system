RHAPSODY — BRAND & DESIGN GUIDELINE (external partner package)
==============================================================

WHAT'S HERE
  Rhapsody-Brand-Guideline.html  Open in any web browser. Fully self-contained
                                 (fonts + images embedded) — works offline, no
                                 internet needed. This is the primary reference.
  Rhapsody-Brand-Guideline.pdf   Same guide as a PDF, for printing or emailing.
  rhapsody-brand-assets/         The approved MASTER ART to build with:
                                   - Rhapsody logos (SVG + PNG, all lockups)
                                   - R monograms
                                   - dot-devices/ : big-dot & scattered-dot
                                     device masters (SVG) + PNG dot fills

HOW TO USE
  1. Start with the guideline (HTML or PDF) for the rules and correct examples.
  2. Use the files in rhapsody-brand-assets/ AS-IS — do not rebuild, recolor,
     or restretch the logos or dot devices. Flip/rotate 90° only where the
     guide says it's allowed.
  3. Fonts: Poppins (headlines/body) and IBM Plex Mono (eyebrows/labels) —
     both free from Google Fonts.
  4. Anything not included here (product logos, the full icon library, light
     device masters) lives in Rhapsody's internal library — request it from
     your Rhapsody contact.

Questions or approvals: your Rhapsody marketing contact.
