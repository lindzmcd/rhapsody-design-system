RHAPSODY EMAIL SIGNATURE — EMPLOYEE SETUP GUIDE
================================================================
If your signature isn't set automatically by IT, follow these steps
so yours matches the company standard. It takes about two minutes.

WHAT YOU NEED
  - The signature template (ask IT, or use signature-standard.html
    from this package).
  - Your name, job title, phone number, and Rhapsody email.

------------------------------------------------------------------
OUTLOOK (new Outlook for Windows / Mac, and Outlook on the web)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Settings (gear) > Accounts > Signatures  (or Mail > Compose and
     reply).
  2. Create a new signature named "Rhapsody".
  3. Open signature-standard.html in a browser, select the whole
     signature, copy it, and paste into the signature box. (Pasting the
     rendered signature keeps the logo, divider, and formatting.)
  4. Replace the placeholders with your details: name, title, phone,
     email.
  5. Set it as the default for New messages and Replies/forwards.

Classic Outlook (older Windows): File > Options > Mail > Signatures >
New, then paste as above.

------------------------------------------------------------------
GMAIL (web)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Settings (gear) > See all settings > General > Signature.
  2. Create a new signature named "Rhapsody".
  3. Open signature-standard.html in a browser, copy the whole
     signature, and paste it into the signature box.
  4. Replace the placeholders with your details.
  5. Under "Signature defaults," select it for new emails and
     replies/forwards. Save Changes at the bottom.

------------------------------------------------------------------
FILL IN THESE FIELDS
------------------------------------------------------------------
  [First Last]            -> your full name
  [Job Title]             -> your job title
  [+1 555 000 0000]       -> your phone number
  [name@rhapsody.health]  -> your Rhapsody email

------------------------------------------------------------------
KEEP IT ON BRAND (please don't change)
------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Don't swap the logo, change the fonts, or recolor anything.
  - No quotes, taglines, banners, background colors, or social icons.
  - Company name is always "Rhapsody," never "Rhapsody Health."
  - Certifications: only if your role requires them. Use the
    "with-certifications" version and ask IT / Security for the official
    badge images, don't add your own.

If the logo doesn't appear, your email is showing images as "off" for
that message, the recipient will still see it once images load. If it
never shows, contact IT, the logo needs to be hosted at a public URL.
