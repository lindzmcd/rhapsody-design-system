The Patient Matching Game

One patient. Twelve records. The clues arrive the way they do in real life: over the phone, by fax, off a card. Flip down everyone who doesn’t fit, and try not to mix up two humans while you’re at it.

Your mission

Work the clues, rule out the records that don’t fit, and lock in your match when one card is left standing. Then we’ll see how it went.

  1. 1Read the clue. It’s exactly what came in, nothing more. How to interpret it is your call.
  2. 2Tap records to flip them down. Tap again to un-flip if you get cold feet.
  3. 3Down to one card? Lock in your match and face the reveal.
