Round 2: it gets harder.

Jayme was one person scattered across systems. The next patient is the opposite problem, and it’s worse.

The part we didn’t tell you

There are two Alex Nguyens. Born the same day.

Round 1 was one person split across many records. This is the opposite failure: two different people whose records are one careless merge away from becoming one. If that happens, your Alex inherits the other Alex’s medication list. It’s called an overlay, and it’s the most dangerous identity error in healthcare.

Nothing on the board could separate them. A match engine that guesses is a coin flip at scale. Rhapsody Identity scores every match, links what belongs together, keeps apart what doesn’t, and flags a pair like this for human review with the evidence attached.
