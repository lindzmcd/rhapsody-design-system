Find the Right Patient

It plays like the classic face-flipping board game, but with worse data and higher stakes. One patient needs you. Nine records could be them. Flip down the records that don’t fit the clues until only one is left.

Your mission

  1. 1Read the clue. It’s exactly what came in, from a phone call, a fax, a card. How to interpret it is your call.
  2. 2Tap any record that doesn’t fit to flip it face-down. Tap again to bring it back.
  3. 3One record left? Lock it in and find out if it’s the right person.
