Axon Connected badge usage guidelines

How vendors display the badge, and what it can and cannot say.

Draft for design and legal review · October 2026

What the badge means

Purpose

The Axon Connected badge lets vendors show health systems that connecting to them is already documented.

The badge shows

  • The vendor has published an integration playbook in Rhapsody Axon™ Broadcast.
  • Health systems can ask Axon how to connect to that vendor and get answers from the playbook.
  • The playbook is visible to the organizations the vendor chose.

The badge does not show

  • That Rhapsody has certified, validated or approved the vendor's product, security or compliance.
  • That Rhapsody endorses the vendor or has a partnership with them.
  • That any integration will be fast, complete or guaranteed.

Who can use it

PublishedThe vendor has a live playbook in Axon Broadcast.
CurrentSpecs and contacts in the playbook are kept up to date.
LinkedThe badge links to the vendor's playbook page.
Removed when neededIf the playbook is unpublished, the badge comes down.

Size and clear space

Keep it readable

Keep clear space around every badge and never shrink it below the minimum size.

Circle. Clear space on every side equals one quarter of the badge width.

Bar. Clear space on every side equals one quarter of the badge height.

BadgeMinimum on screenMinimum in print
Circles64 px wide0.75 in wide
Bars160 px wide1.5 in wide

Also not allowed

Added in this version

These rules cover how vendors use the badge in their own materials.

Edit or crop itDo not change the wording, remove the R mark or trim any part.
Redraw or recreate itAlways use the supplied files.
Merge it into your logoDo not combine it with your logo or other badges as one mark.
Use it as a product nameIt is a status mark, not a brand or product name.
Put it on photos or patternsUse a solid panel behind it.
Place text or graphics over itKeep the clear space free.
Animate itDo not spin, pulse, fade or bounce it.
Imply certification or endorsementSee the claims page for approved wording.
Keep it up after unpublishingRemove it if your playbook is no longer live.
Use it for other productsIt covers the playbook it links to, nothing else.

Claims and naming

What you can say

The badge says a playbook is published. Keep the words around it just as specific.

Approved wording

“Our integration playbook is published in Rhapsody Axon™ Broadcast.”
“Health systems can ask Axon how to connect with us.”
“Find our playbook in the Directory.”

Do not say

“Rhapsody certified” or “Rhapsody approved”
“Validated by Axon”
“Guaranteed integration” or “instant connection”
  • First mention in any text is “Rhapsody Axon™.” Later mentions can use “Axon.”
  • Company name is “Rhapsody,” never “Rhapsody Health.”
  • The badge artwork reads “Axon Connected.” Legal to confirm any trademark line vendors must add beside it.
