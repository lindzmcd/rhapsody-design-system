Axon Connected badge usage guidelines

How vendors display the badge, and what it can and cannot say.

October 2026

What the badge means

Purpose

The Axon Connected badge lets vendors show health systems that connecting to them is already documented.

The badge shows

  • The vendor has published an integration playbook in Rhapsody Axon™ Broadcast.
  • Health systems can ask Axon how to connect to that vendor and get answers from the playbook.
  • The playbook is visible to the organizations the vendor chose.

The badge does not show

  • That Rhapsody has certified, validated or approved the vendor's product, security or compliance.
  • That Rhapsody endorses the vendor or has a partnership with them.
  • That any integration will be fast, complete or guaranteed.

Conditions of use

PublishedThe vendor has a live playbook in Axon Broadcast.
CurrentSpecs and contacts in the playbook are kept up to date.
LinkedThe badge links to the vendor's playbook page.
Taken down if unpublishedRemove the badge as soon as the playbook is no longer live.

03

Contrast, space and size

Keep it readable

Pick the badge that contrasts with its background, give it room, and keep it above the minimum size.

Choose by contrast

Use a dark badge on a light background and a light badge on a dark one. If the background is a photo or pattern, set the badge on a solid panel.

Light background Navy or Blue badge

Light gray Blue badge

Dark background White badge

Clear space and size

Circle. Keep clear space on every side equal to one quarter of the badge width.

Bar. Keep clear space on every side equal to one quarter of the badge height.

Minimum size: circles 64 px wide on screen or 0.75 in in print. Bars 160 px wide on screen or 1.5 in in print.

Correct use

Good examples

Three habits keep the badge consistent everywhere it appears.

Use the official file. Scale it proportionally and keep its original colors.

Match the badge to the ground. Use a white version on navy.

Give it room. Keep clear space and set it apart from other logos.

Also not allowed

More restrictions

These rules cover how vendors use the badge in their own materials.

Edit or crop itDo not change the wording, remove the R mark or trim any part.
Redraw or recreate itAlways use the supplied files.
Merge it into your logoDo not combine it with your logo or other badges as one mark.

Imply certification or endorsementSee page 9 for approved wording.
Keep it up after unpublishingRemove it if your playbook is no longer live.
Use it for other productsIt covers the playbook it links to, nothing else.
08

Claims and naming

What you can say

The badge says a playbook is published. Keep the words around it just as specific.

Approved wording

“Our integration playbook is published in Rhapsody Axon™ Broadcast.”
“Health systems can ask Axon how to connect with us.”
“Find our playbook in the Directory.”

Do not say

“Rhapsody certified” or “Rhapsody approved”
“Validated by Axon”
“Guaranteed integration” or “instant connection”
  • First mention in any text is “Rhapsody Axon™.” Later mentions can use “Axon.”
  • Company name is “Rhapsody,” never “Rhapsody Health.”
  • The badge artwork reads “Axon Connected.” Use it exactly as supplied.
09

Files and approval

Getting the badge

Vendors request the kit, Rhapsody confirms the playbook, and the files arrive by email.

RequestVendor submits company, work email and playbook link.
ConfirmRhapsody checks the playbook is published.
ReceiveKit arrives with files and these guidelines.
MaintainVendor keeps the playbook current and removes the badge if it is unpublished.

In the kit

  • All five badges as SVG
  • All five badges as PNG, at 1x and 2x
  • These guidelines as a PDF

Changes and removal

  • Rhapsody may update or retire a badge and will notify vendors.
  • Rhapsody may ask a vendor to remove the badge if it is used outside these guidelines.
