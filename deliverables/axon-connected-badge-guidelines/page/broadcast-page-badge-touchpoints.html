What is the Axon Connected badge and who can use it?

The Axon Connected badge shows health systems that your integration Playbook is published in Rhapsody Axon™, so they know how to connect to you. Any vendor with a published Playbook can request the badge kit, which includes the files and usage guidelines. If your Playbook is unpublished, the badge should come down.
