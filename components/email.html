Email

Email uses the same system as the web: a navy header band carrying the wordmark and a Pulse glow, a teal seam where the header meets the body, then black or navy copy on white for maximum legibility. Keep one message and one primary action per send.

  • Header. Navy band with the white wordmark and an optional Pulse glow offset to one side. Add the program or series name (for example, “The Modern Integrator”) in light blue beneath the wordmark. Close the band with the teal seam.
  • Body. Black or navy body copy on white, Poppins, generous line spacing. Glows and dot fields stay in the header, not behind body text.
  • One CTA. A single primary button, teal on the light body. Secondary links can be inline text in Rhapsody Blue.
  • Imagery. Follow the photography rules; portraits use the thin-ring treatment. Keep the clear margin around any graphic.
  • Accessibility. Maintain the 3:1 minimum contrast; don’t set long copy in light blue or teal on white.
