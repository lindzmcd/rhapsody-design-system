Typography

Two typefaces carry the system. Poppins sets headlines and body copy; IBM Plex Mono sets eyebrows and technical annotations. Load both from Google Fonts and reference them through the design tokens.

Type scale

Headlines lean light (Poppins 300–400); weight comes from size and space, not bold. Body is Poppins 300–400.

  • All caps, always.
  • IBM Plex Mono Medium (500). Regular 400 is acceptable where 500 renders heavy at very small sizes.
  • Size roughly 12–13px, sitting above the headline with clear space between.
  • Letter-spacing about 0.16 to 0.18em so the caps do not look cramped.
  • Rhapsody Blue on light backgrounds, teal on dark grounds.
  • Markup: a <p> or <span>, never a heading tag.
  • Avoid: Poppins for eyebrows (retired), bold weight, tight or absent letter-spacing, and purple or orange for the eyebrow.

Technical annotations (IBM Plex Mono)

Use IBM Plex Mono for code, field names, versions, IDs, and similar technical strings, so they read as machine text distinct from prose.

Implementation: load https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500&display=swap and reference it via the token --mono: "IBM Plex Mono", monospace;.

Dates, times, and name lockups

Dates and times

  • Dates: abbreviated month, no period: Jan 4, 2026. Ranges use a hyphen with no spaces: Jan 1-4, 2026.
  • Times: lowercase a.m./p.m. with periods, hyphenated range, time zone after: 10-11 a.m. EST.
  • Multiple time zones separate with a pipe: 10-11 a.m. EST | 9-10 a.m. CST.

Order is always name, then title, then company, each on its own line.

  • On dark, use the event card pattern: eyebrow, session title, date/time line, then the lockup.

Headline color combination (dark backgrounds)

On navy or dark backgrounds, set a two-part headline in white plus light blue, the emphasis word in light blue #90C5FF and the rest in white. This is the release-notes banner treatment. On light backgrounds use navy with a blue accent word instead.

Minimum font size (web)

  • Body copy: at least 16px on web. This is the baseline for comfortable reading.
  • Secondary and supporting text: at least 14px.
  • Captions, labels, and fine print: at least 12px. Do not go below 12px for anything a user needs to read.
  • Legal or disclaimer text: at least 11px, used only where unavoidable.
  • Maintain at least 3:1 contrast for large text and 4.5:1 for normal body text, so size and contrast work together.
