Quotes & testimonials

Proof points work hardest when they are short. Cut quotes to a single powerful snippet and stats to the takeaway, then let the number or the line carry the weight. Set the snippet or stat large in a brand color, and name the customer in small text rather than dropping in a logo, logos at different shapes and colors create visual chaos.

Quotes

  • Reduce to one short, powerful line. Cut everything that is not the point.
  • Set it large in a brand color, following the type and color rules.
  • Highlight in a Rhapsody-colored box with white text, or Rhapsody-colored text on white.
  • Attribute with the customer name in small text, not a logo.
  • Always wrap the quote copy in opening and closing quotation marks. An oversized decorative mark is an optional accent, not a substitute for them.
  • Length caps: one-line quote snippets stay under 60 characters; longer testimonials under 120. If a quote needs more, it needs editing.

A longer testimonial sets the quote on navy, in opening and closing marks, with a soft serif oversized mark as the accent, then attributes it with the name lockup: name, title, company. A soft Aurora glow offset to the calm side gives the card depth: the light device, never behind the copy. Keep the quote itself tight.
