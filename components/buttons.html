Buttons and CTAs

Buttons are pill-shaped, set in Poppins Medium (500), sentence case, with a short action label like “Learn more” or “Request a demo.” Keep one primary button per view. Use a secondary outline button for a lower-priority action.

The pill shape

The pill is fully rounded: the corner radius equals half the button’s height, so the left and right ends are perfect semicircles. In CSS this is border-radius: 999px (any value at or above half the height produces the same full curve).

Primary and secondary

Primary is a filled pill and there is one per view: teal on dark grounds, blue on light. Secondary is the same pill as an outline, for a lower-priority action: white on dark, blue on light.

  • Fully rounded pill: border-radius: 999px (radius = half the height). Never a partial-radius rounded rectangle.
  • Poppins Medium (500), sentence case, no bold (700).
  • One primary button per view; secondary actions use the outline style.
  • Labels are short and action-led, and stay consistent through a flow.
  • Primary fill is teal on dark, blue on light. Keep to no more than three brand colors in the surrounding layout.
  • Give buttons clear padding and space; do not place dots or graphics over them.

Text links and the CTA arrow

Secondary and tertiary text links carry a trailing arrow. Pill buttons never do.

  • Applies to secondary and tertiary text links: “Explore …”, “Meet …”, “Learn more”, card CTAs, and inline navigational links.
  • Does not apply to primary or secondary pill buttons. Buttons never take an arrow.
  • Glyph: the right-arrow character &rarr; ( → ), not ->, », or an icon font. One arrow per link, always trailing, never leading.
  • Color: the link inherits its context color: blue on light infrastructure contexts, teal (#23C5BF) on light automation contexts, white or light on dark grounds. The arrow matches the link text color. Never orange.
  • Motion (optional): the arrow may nudge 2–3px to the right on hover; keep it subtle.
