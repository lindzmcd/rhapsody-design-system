Launch (Burst): energy from the core, offset right; product name and copy on the calm left. Example: a Rhapsody Axon launch.

  • Product name leads, set in the name lockup (with the ® where registered), followed by one plain-language line on what it does.
  • Burst on navy. One light device, Burst, offset into open space with copy on the calm side; light devices are navy-only. Keep the glow within teal, blue, and purple.
  • One CTA. A single action (a teal or Rhapsody Blue pill) plus the product URL; no competing buttons.
  • Keep it tight. Name, one-line promise, one support sentence, one CTA—nothing more; let the glow carry the energy.
  • Formats. Design the 16:9 for web heroes, slides, and email headers; hold the wordmark and copy inside a safe margin.
