Quick corrections log

Direction confirmed, superseding earlier drafts

  • Multicolor dots are approved. Both white-on-blue and multicolor are equal, on-brand registers for digital and physical. An earlier draft wrongly labeled multicolor off-brand.
  • Dots are always structured. The booth “flow” is a size-graduated grid, not a random scatter. Random placement is the thing to avoid.
  • Squiggle and icon-on-circle illustrations are retired for new work, even though both appear in the brand guide.
  • The heavy photo color-ring is retired. Portraits now use the modern treatments (thin ring, arc accent, card, or editorial split), which supersede the thick-ring application from the earlier brand guide.
  • Photography: bright and natural-lit. Avoid dark imagery, blur, and harsh light flaring at the camera.
  • Iconography moved to a single outline family (adopted from the homepage prototype, July 2026): stroke-only, 1.7 weight, currentColor, 44px gray tile for feature icons; check/cross functional marks at weight 3. The two-color line icons are superseded for new work.
  • New approved treatments added: bracket logo treatment, dot row accent, white-plus-light-blue headline on dark, and the teal separator line.
  • Module accent line (adopted from the homepage prototype, July 2026): a 44×3px, 2px-radius bar above each H2 module: blue for build contexts, teal for automation, never orange; one per module, never full-width.
  • Punctuation: em dashes are avoided wherever another correct option exists; colons, semicolons, commas, parentheses, or a new sentence take their place.
  • Copy length caps (July 2026): hero headline ≤40 characters, hero supporting line ≤110, quote snippets ≤60, testimonials ≤120.
  • Eyebrows and technical annotations moved to IBM Plex Mono (July 2026), retiring the Poppins Medium uppercase eyebrow treatment.
  • Date, time, and name-lockup formats added (July 2026). Jan 4, 2026 · 10-11 a.m. EST | 9-10 a.m. CST · name / title / company stacked lockup.
