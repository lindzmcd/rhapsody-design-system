Quick corrections log

Latest updates (July 2026)

  • Logo bar is now Rhapsody Navy, locked in. The primary lockup bar is #0B2C47 (not black), baked into the artwork so it cannot revert.
  • New dark-background lockup: blue bar with a white wordmark, the inverse of the old white-bar/blue-word version. It is the standard on navy, where an all-blue wordmark was hard to read.
  • Light blue refreshed to #B4D8FF (lighter, less saturated), replacing #90C5FF across the system.
  • Text on teal: use navy or black only, never white (white is 2.1:1 and fails at any size). Minimum 16px body, 14px labels, 12px floor. See Color.
  • The type hierarchy is one weight, Poppins Regular (400). H2–H4 and body copy all set in Regular; the hero and H1 step up to Medium (500), which also carries short standalone “moment” headlines. See Typography.
  • Two-tone headline is for emphasis only: color just the highlighted word(s); the rest of the headline and the description stay white or black per background. Use sparingly.
  • Graduated dot field retired. The uniform scattered field is the single dot pattern for backgrounds.
  • Module accent-line color stays consistent across an activation: pick blue or teal and hold it across every slide/surface; don’t switch mid-set.
  • Employee email signature added. See Email for the wordmark, teal divider, name lockup, and contact lines.

Direction confirmed, superseding earlier drafts

  • Multicolor dots are approved. Both white-on-blue and multicolor are equal, on-brand registers for digital and physical. An earlier draft wrongly labeled multicolor off-brand.
  • Dots are always structured. Apply the uniform scattered field (equal-size dots, densest at an edge and thinning inward), never a random scatter. The size-graduated grid is retired; see Primary graphic devices.
  • Squiggle and icon-on-circle illustrations are retired for new work, even though both appear in the brand guide.
  • The heavy photo color-ring is retired. Portraits now use the modern treatments (thin ring, arc accent, card, or editorial split), which supersede the thick-ring application from the earlier brand guide.
  • Photography: bright and natural-lit. Avoid dark imagery, blur, and harsh light flaring at the camera.
  • Iconography moved to a single outline family (adopted from the homepage prototype, July 2026): stroke-only, 1.7 weight, currentColor, 44px gray tile for feature icons; check/cross functional marks at weight 3. The two-color line icons are superseded for new work.
  • New approved treatments added: bracket logo treatment, dot row accent, white-plus-light-blue headline on dark, and the teal separator line.
  • Module accent line (adopted from the homepage prototype, July 2026): a 44×3px, 2px-radius bar above each H2 module: blue for build contexts, teal for automation, never orange; one per module, never full-width.
  • CTA arrow on text links (adopted from the homepage prototype, July 2026): secondary/tertiary text links end with a trailing →; buttons never take an arrow. Teal (#23C5BF) is the actionlink color in automation contexts, an approved exception to “no teal copy.”
  • Copy length caps (July 2026): hero headline ≤40 characters, hero supporting line ≤110, quote snippets ≤60, testimonials ≤120.
  • Brand Identity Evolution approved and folded in: the light-based graphic devices (Aurora, the Aurora ribbon, Burst, and Plexus; Pulse and the Aurora-Burst hybrid retired; formerly grouped as “Radiance”) and the modern real-people headshot treatments (thin ring, arc accent, rounded-rectangle card, editorial split), with the thick color-ring retained for representative/editorial imagery.
  • Eyebrows and technical annotations moved to IBM Plex Mono (July 2026), retiring the Poppins Medium uppercase eyebrow treatment. See Typography.
  • Date, time, and name-lockup formats added (July 2026). See Typography for the formats: Jan 4, 2026 · 10-11 a.m. EST | 9-10 a.m. CST · name / title / company stacked lockup.
