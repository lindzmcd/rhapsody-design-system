Display ads

Display units are billboard-simple: Rhapsody Blue leads (it reads best in display; navy is the occasional alternate), the logo is prominent, a few large words carry one quick read, and there is one action. The full system is available, but restraint wins the placement. One in-context example is shown below; exhaustive per-size placement specs live with the paid-media team, not in this guideline.

In context

  • Blue-first ground. Rhapsody Blue is the default; navy is the occasional alternate. White logo on both.
  • Logo leads, always. The wordmark is prominent in every unit; use the monogram only where the wordmark won’t fit (≤50px tall). Never small or buried.
  • Few, large words. Billboard logic, a short headline read in seconds. Cut words before you shrink type; 13px floor at delivered size.
  • One device, one action. One device max, photo, fluid dot pattern, big dot, or nothing, clear of the copy, and a single CTA (a pill, or an arrow chip in tight formats).
Keep it to one read

If a unit needs more than a glance, it’s doing too much. Trim to the logo, one line, and one action; let the blue ground and a single device do the rest.
