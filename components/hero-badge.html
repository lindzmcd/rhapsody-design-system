Hero badge graphics

The hero badge is Rhapsody’s signature treatment for website heroes, product pages, and market-segment pages. It takes the dots device and resolves it into a radiating halo around a navy disc held by a thick Rhapsody-Blue ring. The disc carries either a person (photo) or a product / segment (illustration), and small icon badges can tag what the system does. It is the same family as the portrait ring, scaled up into a full brand moment.

Built from the dots

The halo is the fluid dot pattern bent into a circle, dense at the ring and fading outward with no hard edge. The badge is a focal device, one per hero, that lets a photo or illustration sit inside the brand system instead of floating on its own.

The master treatment

Established on the website heroes. Treat it as the reference for ring weight, the navy band, the halo falloff, and badge placement.

Anatomy

  • Ring. One circle: navy fill (#0B2C47) with a Rhapsody-Blue stroke (#1A81F4). The stroke reads as a bold navy band with an ~8px blue outer edge. Keep it even all the way around.
  • Fill. A circular crop centered in the disc. For photos, leave a navy band (~10% of the photo diameter) between the photo edge and the ring. For illustrations, the artwork sits directly on the navy.
  • Dot halo. Light blue (#B4D8FF) half-tone dots on a light ground, dense next to the ring and fading out radially — never a square or cropped edge.
  • Icon badges (optional). Small circles on the ring that tag a capability. Two per badge at most, placed at opposite corners, clear of the face.

Two fills

One badge system, two jobs. Photos carry people, customers, teams, and homepage heroes. Illustrations carry products and market segments, a single white-and-light-blue illustration centered on the navy disc.

Icon badges

Optional capability tags on the ring. Use at most two, in the two weights below, to signal a feature (security, exchange, automation). Keep them off faces and clear of the halo’s densest dots.

Do
  • Keep the ring even and the halo fading softly to nothing.
  • One badge per hero; photo for people, illustration for products and segments.
  • Center the fill; give a photo its navy band so it doesn’t touch the ring.
  • Hold icon badges to two, at opposite corners, clear of the face.
Don’t
  • Don’t crop the halo to a square or let it end in a hard edge.
  • Don’t recolor the ring; it stays navy with the blue edge.
  • Don’t crowd the disc with more than one subject or stack many badges.
  • Don’t mix a photo and an illustration inside one badge.
Master-art rule

Use the approved hero-badge master art (photo rings and segment illustrations) as provided. Recreate the ring and halo only from the spec above when a live element is genuinely needed.
