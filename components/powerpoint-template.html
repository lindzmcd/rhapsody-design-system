PowerPoint deck template

The 2026 Rhapsody PowerPoint template (November 2026) is the reference for every Rhapsody slide and deck, whether built in PowerPoint or designed in Claude. It is still a work in progress, but use it today. Find it in the PPT Template folder on SharePoint (Marketing > Private > Graphic Design > Projects > 2026 > 2026 Rebrand > PPT Template > Rhapsody PowerPoint Template - 2026 KASS).

Two parts are still being replaced

Dots: the dot artwork in the template is the old version. Use the fluid dot pattern from the Graphic Devices library instead (see Fluid dot). Icons: the icons in the template are placeholders. Use the FY27 icon library instead (see Icons). A designer is updating both in the template. Until then, never copy the template’s dots or icons into new work.

Using it

  • In PowerPoint: start every deck from the template, not a blank file. Pick the layout that fits the content and fill it in. Don’t change the colors, fonts, or structure.
  • In Claude: slides and decks follow the same layouts, type, footer, and colors as the template. Swap in the fluid dots and FY27 icons wherever the template has the old dots or icons.
  • Poppins Medium for headings and Poppins Regular for body; IBM Plex Mono for eyebrows and labels.
  • The all-white logo on navy, Aurora Glow over navy, and Rhapsody Blue. The primary logo on white and light backgrounds.
  • Keep to three brand colors per slide, and use the rounded-bottom container for one element per slide at most.
  • Keep content inside the slide safe margin of about 0.4–0.5in (see Spacing).
