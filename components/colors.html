Color

Rhapsody Blue and Rhapsody Navy are the primary colors, used most prominently. Light blue and teal are secondary. Purple and orange are tertiary accents, used sparingly. Backgrounds stay white, gray, or blue.

Text color

  • Copy uses only black, white, or the primary colors (Rhapsody Blue and Rhapsody Navy).
  • On light backgrounds: text in black or navy, with Rhapsody Blue as the accent.
  • On dark backgrounds: text in white, with light blue as the accent (its dark-background counterpart to blue).
  • Teal, purple, and orange are not used for copy. Exception: text links in automation contexts may use teal (#23C5BF); see Buttons and CTAs.
  • The one exception: an H3 heading may take a secondary or tertiary color. Body copy, captions, and larger headings (H1, H2) stay within the colors above.
  • Minimum 3:1 contrast. Digital uses HEX or RGB; print uses CMYK or Pantone.
  • Gray-blue (#E8EDF2) is a neutral for subtle background fills such as alternating table rows.
The three-color rule, and its exceptions

Keep any single layout to no more than three brand colors. The dot pattern is exempt (it may run multicolor, including a few orange dots), and functional UI marks (the check and cross) sit outside the count.

Color accessibility

Type and background combinations must meet a minimum 3:1 contrast ratio. Checking contrast is always your responsibility. A good rule of thumb: black or white type plus one other color.
