Illustrations

The previous character-driven illustration style is retired for new work. It does not support the more mature, technology-forward position Rhapsody needs with enterprise buyers, partners, and investors.

Prefer
  • Real photography and credible technical environments.
  • Product/interface imagery and software shown in context.
  • Data visualization, architecture diagrams, and system maps.
  • Approved graphic devices.
Avoid
  • Cartoon people, whimsical scenes, cute metaphors, or character-driven spot illustrations.
  • Generic robot heads, glowing brains, humanoid AI figures, circuit-board faces, or hologram clichés.
  • Decorative sparkles or “AI magic” motifs used without a specific brand-system reason.
