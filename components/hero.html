  • Length caps: hero headline no more than 40 characters; supporting line no more than 110 characters (about two short lines). If it doesn’t fit, cut the copy, not the type size.
  • Eyebrow in IBM Plex Mono (blue on light, teal on dark), headline in Poppins Medium (500) with one accent word, one pill button.
  • One visual only: a single device offset right and clear of the copy, or a photo with a scrim behind the type. Never a device and a photo together.
