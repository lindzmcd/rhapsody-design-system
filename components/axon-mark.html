Rhapsody Axon mark

The robot character is the mark for Rhapsody Axon™, the product’s own visual, as on the homepage hero and the Rhapsody Axon AI bands. It is not Teddie Mercury, and it is not a Rhapsody graphic device. Use the files as provided; never recreate or alter them. They are in the Axon Character Mark library on SharePoint (Marketing > Private > Graphic Design > _Asset Library > Characters > Axon Character Mark).

Versions

Full color axon-mark-fullcolor
The primary mark, on white or light backgrounds.
Navy axon-mark-navy
One color, on light backgrounds.
Reversed axon-mark-reversed
On navy and dark backgrounds.
White axon-mark-white
One color, on dark backgrounds and on Rhapsody Blue.

Backgrounds

Full color on light. White or light backgrounds.
Reversed on dark. Navy or dark backgrounds.
White on Rhapsody Blue. One-color white on color.

Always keep strong contrast between the mark and its background. If any part of the mark is hard to see, switch to the other version. Never use the full-color mark on a dark background.

Files and formats

FormatUse for
PNG, transparent, 16–1024pxPresentations, documents, and email
JPG, solid backgroundOnly where transparency isn’t supported
SVGWeb, print, and large formats; scales with no loss of quality
Animated states (GIF, Lottie, JSON, SVG)Product and web motion: idle, thinking, working, reading, success, offline, in full color and reversed (02 Animated States folder). Use the state that matches what the product is doing.

The 512px PNGs are also in this design system under assets/axon-mark/; take every other size, the SVGs, and the animations from the library.

Clear space

Keep clear space around the whole mark, including the antenna, as shown in the Axon logo usage guidelines. Don’t crowd it with other logos or text.

Do
  • Use the provided files, in the version that matches the background.
  • Keep strong contrast and clear space.
  • Use it for Rhapsody Axon only, alongside the Rhapsody Axon™ name rules.
Don’t
  • Change the colors, stretch or squish, or rotate it.
  • Add shadows or effects, or crop or rearrange its parts.
  • Place it on busy or low-contrast backgrounds, crowd it with other logos or text, or use the full-color mark on dark backgrounds.

Source: Axon Logo Usage Guidelines (November 2026), in examples/brand/ in the design system repository.
