Graphic devices at a glance

Rhapsody works from one connected family of visual devices. Each carries a distinct meaning and a recommended color lead, so the device reinforces the message rather than just decorating it. Use one device per layout. The color leads below are the natural association and the reasoning behind it, not a lock: the exact blend stays flexible within the brand palette and color hierarchy (see Color). Full specs live in each device’s own card.

Device Meaning Color lead Why / when to use
Aurora When everything comes together, harmony across the system. Teal-led, fading to blue. Teal is the calm, human secondary. Use for connected-care, ease, and automation moments. Navy or dark grounds only.
Aurora ribbon Motion, AI, fluid. Teal → blue → purple. The one place all three flow together; suggests AI without being literal. Video, animated headers, and large backdrops where it isn’t hugging copy.
Plexus Connectivity, the connected platform. Blue-led with teal nodes. Blue is the reliable, connected platform. Use where connection is the point: testimonials, platform and connectivity sections. Navy or dark grounds only.
Burst Breakthrough, launch. Purple-led at the core, fading to blue. Purple is the “something new” energy. Use for launches and AI-forward moments. Navy or dark grounds only.
Dot field Scale and order, data resolving into one connected field. Per background: multicolor or single (Rhapsody blue ground = white only). The precise, infrastructure register. Larger canvases with room to breathe. White, blue, or navy grounds.
Big dot Relationship and momentum, systems of every size connecting. Solid brand color, never orange. The warm, human register. Small, fast-read formats and hero or brand moments.
Photography The human industry we serve, people first. Real imagery; thin-ring accents in primary or secondary colors. A co-equal brand expression, not decoration. Lead with people at least as often as a device: proof, teams, customers. Scrim behind copy.
Reading the table

One device per layout, never two, and never a glow with a dot device or a device over photography. Glows (Aurora, Aurora ribbon, Plexus, Burst) sit on navy or dark grounds only; the dot field and big dot work on white, Rhapsody blue, or navy; photography carries a scrim behind any copy. Color leads are recommendations with a rationale, hold to the palette and hierarchy, and let contrast and the surface decide the exact blend.
