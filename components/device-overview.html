Graphic devices at a glance

Rhapsody works from one connected family of visual devices. Each carries a distinct meaning and a recommended color lead, so the device reinforces the message rather than just decorating it.

The device system is intentionally limited. Fewer choices create a more recognizable, premium brand and make AI-generated work more consistent. Use one visual expression per layout: photography or one approved device, never a stack of devices.

Device Meaning Why / when to use
Aurora When everything comes together, harmony across the system. Use for moments that communicate harmony, ease, and connected systems. Best for brand storytelling, automation, and integrated-care themes. Use on navy or dark backgrounds only.
Aurora ribbon Motion, AI, fluid. Use when movement, transformation, or AI-enabled workflows are the focus. Best for motion, animated headers, launches, and large-format backgrounds. Avoid placing behind dense copy.
Plexus Connectivity, the connected platform. Use when the story is specifically about connectivity, interoperability, networks, or the Rhapsody platform. Best for platform messaging, technical storytelling, and testimonials related to connection. Use on navy or dark backgrounds only.
Burst Breakthrough, launch. Use to signal innovation, launches, breakthroughs, or significant new capabilities. Best reserved for high-impact moments, particularly AI and product announcements. Use on navy or dark backgrounds only.
Dot field Scale and order, data resolving into one connected field. Use to communicate data, infrastructure, scale, and systems working together. Best for larger compositions where the graphic can occupy its own visual space without competing with content.
Big dot Relationship and momentum, systems of every size connecting. Use for simple, high-impact brand moments where a full graphic field would be too complex. Best for small or fast-read formats, hero moments, and compositions that need a strong visual anchor.
Photography The human industry we serve, people first. Use when people, expertise, collaboration, or real-world technology environments strengthen the story. Photography should make Rhapsody feel credible, modern, and human while balancing the more abstract graphic devices.
Master-art rule

Do not recreate or alter graphic devices. Use the approved SVG/PNG master art as provided.

One expression per layout

Never place a device over photography or use multiple devices to fill space. If a layout feels empty, preserve the open space rather than introducing another decorative element.
