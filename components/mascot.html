Mascot: Teddie Mercury

Teddie Mercury is the Rhapsody mascot, a nod to the rock legend behind “Bohemian Rhapsody” and a reminder that serious work doesn’t have to be dull. Use him sparingly and on purpose: he is an accent for culture, event, and swag moments, never a default element, and never a stand-in for the logo.

Draft for review

Based on the Rhapsody brand voice guidelines. Confirm before treating these as final.

Not the Rhapsody Axon mark

The robot character at the center of the homepage hero and on the Rhapsody Axon AI feature bands is the Rhapsody Axon mark, the product’s own visual. It is not Teddie, and the mascot rules below don’t apply to it (see Web pages).

Where Teddie appears

  • Swag and merchandise.
  • SKO and internal culture moments.
  • Customer events, including the Connect Developer Conference.
  • Sales incentives and team campaigns.

Where he never goes

  • Product and web content: product messaging, positioning, technical content, product web pages, and feature images.
  • Credibility content: clinical, security and compliance, regulatory, executive, PR, analyst, and financial material.
  • Proposals, contracts, and customer stories.
  • Next to a product claim or lockup, such as beside Rhapsody Axon™ in a capability statement.
  • Anywhere he would replace, crowd, or compete with the Rhapsody logo.

How he sits on the page

  • One Teddie per asset. He is an accent, not a pattern.
  • He counts as the layout’s one graphic device, so don’t pair him with the dots or Aurora.
  • Outside the logo’s clear space (X, the height of the R), and never combined with the logo into a single lockup.
  • Master art only. Don’t redraw, recolor, re-pose, crop, or add props or speech bubbles.

Name and voice

  • Spelled Teddie, never “Teddy.” Use “Teddie Mercury” on first mention and “Teddie” after.
  • When including him is a judgment call, ask before adding him.
Do
  • Use him for swag, culture, events, and team campaigns.
  • Keep one Teddie per asset, from the approved art.
  • Let him be the layout’s one visual accent.
Don’t
  • Add him to product, web, clinical, security, executive, PR, analyst, or proposal content.
  • Put him beside a product claim, in a logo lockup, or in the logo’s clear space.
  • Confuse him with the Rhapsody Axon mark, or pair him with dots or Aurora.

Teddie master art isn’t in this design system yet; request it from Rhapsody.
