Mascot: Teddie Mercury

Teddie Mercury is the Rhapsody character mark, a nod to the rock legend behind “Bohemian Rhapsody” and a reminder that serious work doesn’t have to be dull. He brings a moment of fun to a small number of appearances. He is not a primary device in the Rhapsody system: the logo, color, type, dots, and Aurora carry the brand, and Teddie shows up only occasionally, on purpose.

Limited appearances

Use Teddie for internal, culture, and Connect Developer Conference materials. Any other use needs approval from the marketing team first.

Approved uses

  • Internal communications: all-hands and SKO materials, internal campaigns, recognition, and employee announcements.
  • Culture moments: team events, celebrations, sales incentives, and employee swag.
  • The Connect Developer Conference: event materials, signage, badges, and conference swag.

Ask the marketing team first

Anything outside those three, such as other customer events, external swag, or social posts, needs marketing approval before Teddie goes in. If you’re not sure, ask; leave him out until it’s approved.

Never

  • Product and web content: product messaging, positioning, product web pages, website feature images, and technical content.
  • Credibility content: clinical, security and compliance, regulatory, legal, executive, PR, analyst, and financial material.
  • Customer documents: proposals, contracts, and customer stories.
  • Next to a product claim or lockup, such as beside Rhapsody Axon™ in a capability statement.
  • As a standing part of the system: slide masters, email signatures, templates, website chrome, or anything that repeats by default.
  • In place of, or combined with, the Rhapsody logo.

How he sits on the page

  • One Teddie per asset, and not on every piece in a set. He is an accent, not a pattern.
  • He counts as the layout’s one graphic device, so don’t pair him with the dots or Aurora.
  • Outside the logo’s clear space (X, the height of the R), and never in a lockup with the logo.
  • Master art only, from the Teddie Mercury character library (Brand Package FY27 > Character Library on SharePoint). Don’t redraw, recolor, re-pose, crop, distort, or add props or speech bubbles.
Not the Rhapsody Axon mark

The robot character at the center of the homepage hero and on the Rhapsody Axon AI feature bands is the Rhapsody Axon mark, the product’s own visual. It is not Teddie, and these rules don’t apply to it (see Web pages).

Name

  • Spelled Teddie, never “Teddy.” Use “Teddie Mercury” on first mention and “Teddie” after.
Do
  • Use him for internal, culture, and Connect Developer Conference pieces.
  • Get marketing approval for anything else.
  • Keep him occasional: one per asset, from the master art.
Don’t
  • Make him a default element, a template fixture, or a campaign’s lead device.
  • Add him to product, web, credibility, or customer documents, or beside a product claim.
  • Pair him with the dots or Aurora, put him in the logo’s clear space, or confuse him with the Rhapsody Axon mark.
