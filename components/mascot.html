Mascot: Teddie Mercury

Teddie Mercury is the Rhapsody mascot, a nod to the rock legend behind “Bohemian Rhapsody” and a reminder that serious work doesn’t have to be dull. On the website he is the friendly face of the Rhapsody Axon™ AI assistant. Use him sparingly and on purpose: he is an accent, never a default element, and never a stand-in for the logo.

Draft for review

Based on how the live website uses Teddie and the Rhapsody brand voice guidelines. Confirm before treating these as final.

Homepage hero (a still from the live animation): Teddie at the center of the constellation, over the Aurora glow. The constellation is reserved for this hero.

Where Teddie appears

  • The homepage hero, at the center of the dot constellation over the Aurora glow (that use only).
  • Rhapsody Axon AI feature bands on navy, as the face of the built-in assistant, with an optional short IBM Plex Mono tag (“Done. Next.”).
  • Culture and event moments: SKO, customer events and the Connect Developer Conference, swag and merchandise, sales incentives and team campaigns.

Where he never goes

  • Credibility content: clinical, security and compliance, regulatory, executive, PR, analyst, and financial material.
  • Proposals, contracts, customer stories, and technical documentation.
  • Inside a product claim or lockup. In a Rhapsody Axon AI band he sits beside the content as the assistant’s face; he never appears in the headline, a capability statement, or a product logo lockup.
  • Anywhere he would replace, crowd, or compete with the Rhapsody logo.

How he sits on the page

  • Navy grounds, over a soft Aurora glow (teal to blue, no hard edge), as on the website. Not on white, Rhapsody Blue, or photography.
  • One Teddie per page or asset. He is an accent, not a pattern.
  • No dot devices. Never with the fluid dot pattern or the big dot; the homepage constellation is the only dots he appears with.
  • On the side opposite the copy, with clear space around him, and noticeably smaller than the headline so he accents the message instead of leading it.
  • Outside the logo’s clear space (X, the height of the R), and never combined with the logo into a single lockup.
  • Master art only. Don’t redraw, recolor, re-pose, crop, or add props or speech bubbles. Animate him only from the approved files (see Light devices → Motion defaults for tempo).

Name and voice

  • Spelled Teddie, never “Teddy.” Use “Teddie Mercury” on first mention and “Teddie” after.
  • His tags are short, upbeat, and plain, set in IBM Plex Mono. He never delivers a product claim or a statistic.
Do
  • Use him for the homepage hero, Rhapsody Axon AI bands, and culture, event, and swag moments.
  • Place him on navy over the Aurora glow, opposite the copy.
  • Keep one Teddie per page or asset, from the approved art.
Don’t
  • Add him to clinical, security, executive, PR, analyst, or proposal content.
  • Put him in a product claim, a logo lockup, or the logo’s clear space.
  • Pair him with the fluid dot pattern or big dot, or redraw or recolor him.

Teddie master art (the hero animation and feature-band files) is available on request from Rhapsody.
