Writing style

Copy follows AP Style (Associated Press) for grammar, punctuation, and capitalization. For full messaging, language rules, and approved copy, see the brand voice and messaging guide.

Avoid em dashes. When another correct option exists, use it: a colon for definitions and lead-ins, a semicolon for linked clauses, commas or parentheses for asides, or a new sentence.

Don’t over-list. Use bullets or numbered lists only when the content is truly a list: discrete items, steps, or specs a reader will scan. Otherwise write prose that states the overall idea in a sentence or two. AI tools especially tend to split one idea into a list of fragments; when that happens, rewrite it as a short paragraph.
