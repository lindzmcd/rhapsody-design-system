Voice

Rhapsody’s voice is trusted, empathetic, strategic, human, and mission-driven. Every word should sound like a person who understands healthcare’s pressures and speaks to impact, not jargon.

Trusted

Customers choose Rhapsody because we deliver consistently and with care. Recognized as Best in KLAS, we’ve earned trust through proven expertise, reliability, and lasting partnerships.

Empathetic

We understand the real pressures our customers face, from regulatory hurdles to resource gaps.

Strategic

We don’t just talk about tech; we speak to the impact. We align solutions with outcomes and communicate without jargon, making complex ideas simple and meaningful.

Human

We are human and approachable: casual, clear, and punchy. Never formal or corporate, always warm, humble, and real.

Mission-driven

We believe in connected care. Every word should reinforce our commitment to making healthcare more interoperable, more secure, and more effective.

Writing style

Copy follows AP Style (Associated Press) for grammar, punctuation, and capitalization. For full messaging, language rules, and approved copy, see the brand voice and messaging guide.

Avoid em dashes. When another correct option exists, use it: a colon for definitions and lead-ins, a semicolon for linked clauses, commas or parentheses for asides, or a new sentence.

Company name is always “Rhapsody”, never “Rhapsody Health”.
