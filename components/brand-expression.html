Brand expression

Rhapsody should feel technologically advanced, intelligent, precise, established, and human. The visual system must communicate innovation without sacrificing the confidence and trust expected of healthcare infrastructure. AI should feel embedded in the system, not represented as novelty or decoration.

Precise, not playful

Favor ordered systems, confident typography, controlled color, and intentional spacing over whimsical or decorative treatments.

Advanced, not sci-fi

Signal modern technology through data, connection, orchestration, product UI, and abstract system imagery rather than futuristic clichés.

Human, not juvenile

Use credible real people and professional environments. Avoid cartoon-like characters, cute metaphors, or illustration styles that reduce enterprise credibility.

Confident, not loud

Let white space, hierarchy, and a restrained palette carry the brand. Do not add color or devices merely to fill open space.
