Brand expression

Rhapsody should feel technologically advanced, intelligent, precise, established, and human. The visual system must communicate innovation without sacrificing the confidence and trust expected of healthcare infrastructure. AI should feel embedded in the system, not represented as novelty or decoration.

Precise, not playful

Favor ordered systems, confident typography, controlled color, and intentional spacing over whimsical or decorative treatments.

Advanced, not sci-fi

Signal modern technology through data, connection, orchestration, product UI, and abstract system imagery rather than futuristic clichés.

Human, not juvenile

Use credible real people and professional environments. Avoid cartoon-like characters, cute metaphors, or illustration styles that reduce enterprise credibility.

Confident, not loud

Let white space, hierarchy, and a restrained palette carry the brand. Do not add color or devices merely to fill open space.

Decision hierarchy

Build every Rhapsody asset by making these calls in order. Each step narrows the next, and an earlier call wins over a later preference.

  1. Ground. White, navy, or Rhapsody Blue; gray-blue only to alternate content modules. See Color.
  2. One lead visual. A real photo, one dot device, or Aurora (navy only). The one sanctioned pairing is a dot device with a photo. See Devices at a glance.
  3. Type. Poppins, an IBM Plex Mono eyebrow, one headline in sentence case, short supporting copy. See Typography.
  4. One accent. Rhapsody Blue first, at most one secondary color; no tertiary color unless a rule authorizes it.
  5. One action. One primary pill per view; text links take the trailing arrow. See Buttons & CTAs.
  6. Space. Hold the clear margin, keep copy clear of the dots, and place nothing over faces, copy, or the logo. If it feels empty, leave it.
Source of truth

Use approved master art verbatim: logos, dot devices, Aurora, and hero badges. Never redraw or restyle it. These section cards are the current rules; where the Quick corrections log or an older guide disagrees, the card wins.

Naming

The company is always Rhapsody: never “Rhapsody Health,” and never possessive (no “Rhapsody’s”; rewrite the sentence instead). Product names and trademarks follow the Rhapsody brand voice guidelines.

Before you ship

  • Every color is on the palette and applied flat; no more than three brand colors (charts and dot devices excepted).
  • Every text-and-ground pair is in the approved color-pair matrix: no teal type on light grounds, and no Rhapsody Blue type on navy (accents on navy are light blue).
  • One lead visual, from approved master art, used as-is.
  • The logo is the right version for its ground, above minimum size, with clear space held.
  • Type: Poppins with a Plex Mono eyebrow, sentence case, no end period on a one-sentence headline, web body at least 16px, nothing a reader needs below 12px.
  • One primary pill; arrows only on text links.
  • Nothing from the retired list (see Quick corrections log).
