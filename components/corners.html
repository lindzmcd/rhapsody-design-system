Corners & shape

Two different rounded shapes do two different jobs, keep them separate. The fully rounded pill is for CTAs only (see Buttons and CTAs). Rounded corners are welcome for framing content, modules, cards, charts, callouts, and image frames, across webpages, PowerPoint slides, and editorial layouts. The recommendation below is how we do that most on brand.

Recommended: square the top, round the bottom

For framing modules and components, square off the top two corners and round the bottom two. The sleekest, most on-brand frame: engineered at the top, settled at the base. Use it as the default frame for content blocks, and keep the treatment consistent across a page or deck.

Module frame

Squared top, rounded bottom (12px).

✓ Recommended

Default for content blocks.

Chart / uniform

All four corners rounded (12px).

✓ Also fine

Charts and dense grids where a uniform frame suits.

Over-rounded

Pill-like radius on a module.

✕ Off-brand

The full pill radius is for CTAs only.

  • Pill (border-radius: 999px) = CTAs only. Never frame a module, card, chart, or image with it.
  • Module frame: square the top two corners, round the bottom two to about 12px (border-radius: 0 0 12px 12px). All four corners rounded (~12px) or fully square is also fine, for example charts and dense grids.
  • One treatment per page or deck, don’t mix squared-top frames with fully rounded ones in the same view.
