Partner logos

Bracket treatment for partner logos

To present a wall of customer or partner logos, place each logo between two curved brackets, echoing the connection motif without hard boxes. This keeps a busy multi-logo grid feeling refined and premium. The parenthesis device is how we bring Rhapsody branding into applications where multiple logos appear, and it also solves for logos of different sizes and shapes (stacked versus horizontal) by giving each one a consistent frame.

  • Two thin single-color arcs, one on each side of the logo, like parentheses.
  • Arc colors are blue, teal, and purple only. One color per logo.
  • Alternate the three colors across the set so no two neighbors match.
  • Place the partner logo in its own colors, centered and clear, between the arcs.
  • Keep arc weight and size consistent across the whole grid.

Before using a partner or customer logo

Using a partner or customer logo is permissible when necessary. Before you place one, verify all of the following:

  • Correct, current version. Confirm it is the most recent, official version of the logo, not an outdated or unofficial mark.
  • Permission. Confirm we have permission to use the logo.
  • High resolution. The file is sharp at its display size, with no pixelation or artifacts.
  • Correct spacing. It sits within the brackets with clear space per guideline; never crowded or stretched.
  • Transparent background. Use a transparent-background file so the logo sits cleanly on the layout, never on its own white or colored box.

On customer logos

The bracket logo treatment is the way to show a wall of logos together. But for a single proof point, a lone logo often clashes with the layout in shape and color. In those cases name the customer in text instead. Reserve logos for the bracket grid where they are sized and colored consistently.
