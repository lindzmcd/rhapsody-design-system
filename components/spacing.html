Layout and spacing

Layouts should always breathe. Two rules keep graphics clean and consistent.

Clear margin (padding)

Keep a clear margin around every graphic of about 6 to 7 percent of the shorter side, with no copy, photo, or logo inside it. For example, roughly a 40 pixel margin on a 600 pixel wide graphic, scaling proportionally. This safe zone keeps content off the edges so the layout always breathes.

Teal separator line

Where a navy band meets the body (web hero, email header), close it with a thin teal seam. It signals the connection motif and separates dark from light without a hard rule.

Module accent line (H2 sections)

A short accent bar sits above each H2 module. It carries the section’s pillar meaning through color and ties section headers, pillar cards, and case cards into one system.

The platform

Build on proven interoperability.

Blue bar: infrastructure / “build” contexts.

Automation

Automate the work around the data.

Teal bar: automation / “automate the work” contexts.

  • Dimensions: 44px wide × 3px tall, 2px corner radius.
  • Spacing: sits above the eyebrow/H2 with about 20–22px below it before the heading.
  • Color carries the section’s pillar meaning: blue (#1A81F4) for infrastructure/“build” contexts, teal (#23C5BF) for automation/“automate the work” contexts. Default to blue when a section is neutral. Never orange.
  • One accent line per module, tied to the primary H2. Do not repeat it on sub-cards within the same module.
  • It is decorative structure, not a divider; don’t stretch it full-width.
