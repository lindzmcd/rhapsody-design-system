Light devices

Alongside the dots, Aurora is the light device, and the only one. Where the dots are precise and rational, Aurora is warm and human, together they say engineered, but human. It is a single device with range: from a calm, soft wash to a flowing band of light. Aurora allows movement, so the same device covers both still and moving work. Use it for photo-free digital moments like heroes, social, slides, and video.

Rules for Aurora

Aurora sits on navy grounds only. On any other background, Rhapsody Blue, white, or a photograph, don’t use a glow, use the dots device or photography instead. The glow uses teal, blue, and purple only, never orange, and stays within three colors. The soft wash may sit behind copy, as on the website hero, as long as the type stays white (light-blue accent) and holds contrast over the glow. Keep the flowing form clear of copy.

One device, soft to flowing

Soft wash. A calm, blended glow. When everything comes together, harmony across the system. The everyday register.

Flowing. A band of light with movement, for motion, video, animated headers, and large hero or event backdrops. The same Aurora, with more energy.

Glow color

Aurora is teal-led and can run through teal, blue, and purple. Within that, the exact blend is left to design, always within the teal / blue / purple glow palette, following the color hierarchy and staying legible on the navy ground.

Movement

Aurora is allowed to move. For motion and large-scale applications, video, animated headers, and large hero or event backdrops, lean into the flowing form, where it has room to move and is not hugging copy. It is a great way to suggest our AI capabilities, modern, fluid, and seamless, without being literal. For everyday layouts, banners, and anywhere text sits close, keep to the calmer wash.

Motion defaults (proposed)

Starting values for animated Aurora, set to the calm tempo of the website hero animation (a 26-second loop). Confirm with the motion or video team before locking them.

PropertyDefault
Loop20–30 seconds, seamless (the website hero runs 26s). Never a visible restart or jump cut.
MovementSlow drift and morph: travel up to about 10% of the frame width per loop, scale between 95% and 105%. No spinning.
EasingEase-in-out (sine) on every move. No bounce, snap, or sudden stop.
ColorShifts stay inside teal, blue, and purple. Never orange, never a hard edge.
BrightnessGentle only, opacity 80–100%. No flashing or strobing.
Behind copyThe soft wash keeps the slowest tempo so text stays readable; the flowing form never moves behind copy.
EntranceFade in over 600–800ms. No zoom or slide-in.
Reduced motionWhen a viewer has reduced motion turned on, show the static soft-wash master instead. Pause the animation when it is off screen.
Video24 or 30fps, seamless loop. Give autoplaying video longer than 5 seconds a pause control.
Web buildAnimate only transform and opacity so motion stays smooth, and keep a hero loop to about 1MB or less.

Aurora in use

Aurora is the atmosphere for the whole frame. On the website hero the soft wash runs right behind the headline and CTA; the banner below shows the same calm wash spreading across a navy frame.

  • Teal, blue, purple only; never orange.
  • Navy grounds only. Digital hero, social, slide, and video atmosphere. Not behind photography, and not a universal print element.
  • Maintain a clear margin of about 6 to 7 percent of the shorter side around the composition.
  • Use one graphic device, dots or Aurora, per layout, not both at once.
