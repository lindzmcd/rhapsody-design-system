Light devices

Alongside the dots, three soft light-based devices carry atmosphere. Where the dots are precise and rational, these are warm and human; together they say engineered, but human. Use them for photo-free digital moments (heroes, social, slides), never behind photography.

Rules for the light devices

Glows use teal, blue, and purple only, never orange (orange lives in the dots and photo-frame rings). Keep each glow within three colors. Offset the glow into open space so copy sits on the calm side of the composition, never on the brightest point.

The three light devices

Device in use

  • Teal, blue, purple only. No orange in glows.
  • Offset the glow; keep copy on the calm side, not the bright center.
  • Digital hero, social, and slide atmosphere. Not behind photography, and not a universal print element.
  • Maintain a clear margin of about 6 to 7 percent of the shorter side around the composition.
  • Use one graphic device, dots or a light device, per layout, not both at once.

Do not use in new applications

Retired for new work
  • The squiggle line. It appears in the brand guide, but we are not using it in any new applications of the brand.
  • Icon-on-circle product illustrations. The bold dimensional illustrations that layer an icon over a filled blue circle are not to be used in new applications. Use the product logo, or the line icons, instead.

If you inherit an older asset that uses either, plan to phase it out rather than extend it.
