Light devices

Alongside the dots, four soft light-based devices carry atmosphere. Where the dots are precise and rational, these are warm and human, together they say engineered, but human. Use them for photo-free digital moments like heroes, social, and slides.

Rules for the light devices

Light devices sit on navy grounds only. On any other background, Rhapsody Blue, white, or a photograph, don’t use a glow, use the dots device or photography instead. Glows use teal, blue, and purple only, never orange. Keep each glow within three colors. Offset the glow into open space so copy sits on the calm side of the composition, never on the brightest point.

The light devices

Aurora: a soft blended wash. When everything comes together, harmony across the system.

Aurora ribbon: a flowing band of light, a second register of Aurora, for motion and large applications (see rule).

Burst: soft rays radiating from a core and fading out. A quiet, atmospheric backdrop for breakthrough moments.

Plexus: a connected network of points, for connectivity moments (see rule).

Glow color

Each device has a recommended color lead (see Devices at a glance): Aurora teal-led, Plexus blue-led, Burst purple-led, and the Aurora ribbon running through all three. Within that, the exact blend is left to design, always within the teal / blue / purple glow palette, following the color hierarchy and staying legible on the navy ground.

When to use Plexus

Plexus is the connected-network device, the most literal expression of interoperability, a network of separate points coming together. Reserve it for moments where connection is the point: behind testimonials and customer quotes, and on platform or connectivity sections. It is not a general-purpose glow; use Aurora or Burst for atmosphere.

When to use the Aurora ribbon

The Aurora ribbon, a flowing band of light, is for motion and large-scale applications, video, animated headers, and large hero or event backdrops, where it has room to move and is not hugging copy. It is a great way to suggest our AI capabilities, modern, fluid, and seamless, without being literal. For everyday layouts, banners, and anywhere text sits close, use the regular Aurora wash instead.
