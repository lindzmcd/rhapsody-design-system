Light devices

Alongside the dots, the Aurora light device carries atmosphere. Where the dots are precise and rational, Aurora is warm and human, together they say engineered, but human. Use it for photo-free digital moments like heroes, social, and slides. Aurora has two registers: the soft wash and the flowing ribbon.

Rules for the light device

Aurora sits on navy grounds only. On any other background, Rhapsody Blue, white, or a photograph, don’t use a glow, use the dots device or photography instead. The glow uses teal, blue, and purple only, never orange. Keep each glow within three colors. Offset the glow into open space so copy sits on the calm side of the composition, never on the brightest point.

The Aurora registers

Aurora (soft wash): a soft blended wash. When everything comes together, harmony across the system. The everyday register.

Aurora ribbon: a flowing band of light, a second register of Aurora, for motion and large applications (see rule).

Glow color

Aurora is teal-led (see Devices at a glance); the Aurora ribbon can run through teal, blue, and purple. Within that, the exact blend is left to design, always within the teal / blue / purple glow palette, following the color hierarchy and staying legible on the navy ground.

When to use the Aurora ribbon

The Aurora ribbon, a flowing band of light, is for motion and large-scale applications, video, animated headers, and large hero or event backdrops, where it has room to move and is not hugging copy. It is a great way to suggest our AI capabilities, modern, fluid, and seamless, without being literal. For everyday layouts, banners, and anywhere text sits close, use the regular Aurora wash instead.
