Web pages (page system)

The refreshed homepage and solutions pages set the template for the whole site. They are built entirely from the existing brand system, Poppins headlines (Medium for the hero, Regular for sections), IBM Plex Mono eyebrows, Rhapsody-Blue pills, and the navy / white / blue / light-gray grounds, so rolling the style to every page is a matter of applying these page-level decisions, not new primitives. Capture notes below; see Colors, Typography, Buttons, and the device cards for the underlying rules.

Section grounds & rhythm

Pages are built from full-width bands that alternate ground color for rhythm. Content bands alternate white and light gray-blue; Rhapsody blue and navy bands are reserved for emphasis (promo, CTA, product/mascot moments). The hero is navy with the Aurora glow (the approved light device, not a hand-built gradient).

Eyebrow & headline

Every section opens the same way: a short blue accent rule, then a mono uppercase eyebrow, then a Poppins Regular headline (24px; only the hero H1 steps up to Medium, 48px) in sentence case, usually ending with a period. Headlines are often two-tone: one phrase in Rhapsody Blue, on navy or white grounds only (see Typography → Two-tone headline). Lowercase mono group labels (“build the infrastructure,” “automate the work”) sub-divide a section.

Devices on the web

  • Homepage-hero constellation (that use only). The homepage hero uses a loose scattered dot constellation (white dots + a few teal, with a faint dotted orbit) around the Teddie Mercury mascot, over the Aurora glow — the one place a dot scatter and Aurora appear together. This is reserved for the homepage hero and nowhere else. It is not a general device: every other page uses the approved fluid dot pattern, big-dot ecosystem, or Aurora, never a loose dot scatter.
  • Mascot + glow. Teddie appears on navy feature bands over a soft Aurora glow (teal→blue, fading to transparent, no hard edge).
  • Hero badges. Product and market-segment tiles use the hero badge graphics (a photo or illustration in a navy disc with the thick blue ring, on the fluid dot pattern) — see that card.
  • Photography heroes. Solutions heroes are a full-bleed photo with a navy scrim graded across the type side (left), the two-tone headline overlaid.
  • Icons. Blue line icons (1.7 stroke, rounded caps) in the standard icon tile for cards; white line-icon chains on emphasis bands. One device per section; dots and glows never combined.
  • Framework visuals are built, not pictured. Concept and architecture diagrams are native, responsive HTML with real text that reflows at every width, never an image inside a screenshot or browser frame. Framed media is reserved for things that genuinely are screens: product UI, demo video, sample deliverables.
