The four slots
  • Eyebrow: section and page, “Markets · Payers”. Times as 12 p.m. CT.
  • Headline: the page H1, sentence case. The emphasis field turns light blue. One sentence takes no period; two or more short sentences take a period after each.
  • Motif: line art from the vocabulary at right.
  • Lockup: a short label, “Rhapsody for payers” or “Rhapsody Axon™”.
Motif vocabulary
  • viewBox 0 0 200 180; keep everything inside x 10–190, y 14–170.
  • Outlines #B4D8FF, stroke 3–3.4 (tiles rx 7–8).
  • Connectors #3D6587, stroke 3.5, drawn first so they sit behind the shapes.
  • Teal #23C5BF for the check badge and at most one other accent.
  • The grounded pulse: stem, dot r8, and ring r14.5 at 45% opacity.
  • Every line ends on a shape or passes fully behind one.
Before you ship
  • Poppins loaded (not Arial), and no line of the headline, eyebrow, or lockup is a single word.
  • Text stays in the left column and the motif in the right; nothing moves out of the layout.
  • Use only the three headline sizes in Setup; don’t invent new ones.
  • Look at the motif up close for stray, floating, or overlapping lines.
  • Export PNG at 2× (2400 × 1260).
